Reflection-Robust 6DoF Object Tracking with Light Fields

ACCV 2026
Nikolai Goncharov, Donald G. Dansereau
Australian Centre for Robotics and School of Aerospace, Mechanical and Mechatronic Engineering, The University of Sydney
{nikolai.goncharov, donald.dansereau}@sydney.edu.au

Fully reflective sugar box. Yellow outline = estimated pose. Flip through the methods.

  • Reflective objects break appearance-based 6DoF trackers: their appearance changes as they mirror the environment.
  • We track from a light field instead, and use the reflections as a pose cue.
  • Each frame is decomposed into geometry, a diffuse albedo, and the environment map it reflects: a relightable surface light field.
  • Pose is refined by relighting under the recovered environment map and minimizing the photometric residual.
  • New dataset: YCBInEOAT re-rendered as light fields at four reflectivity levels. Ours is the only method that holds its accuracy on fully reflective objects.

Method

  • Segment the object, estimate depth robustly against reflections, back-project to points and normals.
  • Every point is seen from all subviews. A dichromatic model splits its colors into a per-point diffuse albedo and one environment map shared by all points.
  • Coarse pose: feature matching on the diffuse colors, with the reflections removed.
  • Refinement: transform the source, relight it under the accumulated environment map, render, and descend the photometric residual.
  • A moving object mirrors new parts of the scene, so the environment map fills in and the signal sharpens over the sequence.

Dataset

We render objects from YCBInEOAT into light fields at multiple reflectivities. Play with the widget.

  • 8 sequences, 4 YCB objects, in two variants: the original mesh and a cube on the same trajectory.
  • Per frame: 5×5 subviews of 640×480 at 5 mm baseline, plus masks, ground truth depth, 6D pose and calibration.
  • Four reflectivity levels r ∈ {0.0, 0.5, 0.7, 1.0}. Geometry, lighting and pose are identical across levels.
  • Simulated RGB-D sensor depth that collapses on shiny surfaces, like a real RealSense.
