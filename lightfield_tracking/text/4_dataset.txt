We render objects from YCBInEOAT<sup><button type="button"
class="cite" data-ref="wen2020">[1]</button></sup> into light fields
at multiple reflectivities.
Play with the widget.

- 8 sequences, 4 YCB objects, in two variants: the original mesh and a cube on
the same trajectory.
- Per frame: 5&times;5 subviews of 640&times;480 at 5&nbsp;mm baseline, plus
masks, ground truth depth, 6D pose and calibration.
- Four reflectivity levels r&nbsp;&isin;&nbsp;{0.0, 0.5, 0.7, 1.0}. Geometry,
lighting and pose are identical across levels.
- Simulated RGB-D sensor depth that collapses on shiny surfaces, like a real
RealSense.
