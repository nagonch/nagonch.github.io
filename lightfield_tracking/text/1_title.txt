[title]
Reflection-Robust 6DoF Object Tracking with Light Fields

[venue]
ACCV 2026

[affiliation]
Australian Centre for Robotics and School of Aerospace, Mechanical and
Mechatronic Engineering, The University of Sydney

[caption]
Flip through the methods to compare tracking quality.

[summary]
- In object tracking, the points between the frames are mainly
identified based on their color.
- Reflective objects break this assumption: their appearance
changes as they move.
- We track from a light field instead, and use the reflections as a pose cue.
- We process the light field image of each frame into a depth
map, surface normals map, albedo image, and the reflected environment
map, resulting in relightable surface light field of the object.
- Pose is refined by relighting under the recovered environment map and
minimizing the photometric residual.
- New dataset: YCBInEOAT<sup><button type="button" class="cite"
data-ref="wen2020">[1]</button></sup> re-rendered as light fields at
four reflectivity
levels. Ours is the only method that holds its accuracy on fully reflective
objects.
