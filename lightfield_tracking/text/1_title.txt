[title]
Reflection-Robust 6DoF Object Tracking with Light Fields

[venue]
ACCV 2026

[affiliation]
Australian Centre for Robotics and School of Aerospace, Mechanical and
Mechatronic Engineering, The University of Sydney

[caption]
Fully reflective sugar box. Yellow outline = estimated pose. Flip through the
methods.

[summary]
- Reflective objects break appearance-based 6DoF trackers: their appearance
  changes as they mirror the environment.
- We track from a light field instead, and use the reflections as a pose cue.
- Each frame is decomposed into geometry, a diffuse albedo, and the
  environment map it reflects: a relightable surface light field.
- Pose is refined by relighting under the recovered environment map and
  minimizing the photometric residual.
- New dataset: YCBInEOAT re-rendered as light fields at four reflectivity
  levels. Ours is the only method that holds its accuracy on fully reflective
  objects.
