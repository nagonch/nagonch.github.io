[heading]
Reflections are a pose signal

[body]
- A moving reflective point changes color as it is re-illuminated, so plain
  color matching fails.
- Monocular color gives only a shallow dip in the loss; raw light field EPIs
  ignore the re-illumination.
- Relit under the environment map, the loss drops to zero at the true motion:
  the reflection carries the pose.
