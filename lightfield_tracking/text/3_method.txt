[heading]
Method

[body]
- We first process our light field into a depth map and a surface normals map
in a way that is robust to specular reflections.
- We then use this geometry to decompose the object's appearance into a
per-point diffuse albedo and an environment map of the surrounding
illumination, shared by all points.
- This results in a surface light field representing the frame. We align the
object in two frames by aligning their surface light fields.
- In practice, we first combine LoFTR<sup><button type="button" class="cite"
data-ref="sun2021loftr">[2]</button></sup> feature matching on the albedos
with colored ICP on the point clouds to find a coarse object transform.
- We then refine the pose: we transform the source surface light field, relight
it under the environment map recovered so far, render it, and minimize the
photometric residual against the observed frame by gradient descent.
- As the object moves, it reflects new parts of the scene, so the environment
map fills in and the relighting signal sharpens over the sequence.
