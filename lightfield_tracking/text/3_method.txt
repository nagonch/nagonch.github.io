[heading]
Method

[body]
- Segment the object, estimate depth robustly against reflections,
  back-project to points and normals.
- Every point is seen from all subviews. A dichromatic model splits its colors
  into a per-point diffuse albedo and one environment map shared by all
  points.
- Coarse pose: feature matching on the diffuse colors, with the reflections
  removed.
- Refinement: transform the source, relight it under the accumulated
  environment map, render, and descend the photometric residual.
- A moving object mirrors new parts of the scene, so the environment map fills
  in and the signal sharpens over the sequence.
